Automation · Dispatch · Operations

Delivery Automation System

Problem

Manual delivery dispatch breaks down at scale — a human has to find, contact, and confirm a rider for every order, with no fallback when someone is unavailable.

Solution

An automated dispatch system that finds available riders, ranks them, offers the delivery, and retries the next rider on rejection — escalating to a human only when every option is exhausted.

Order → Find available riders → Rank riders → Offer delivery → Accepted? → Yes: assign · No: try next rider → All exhausted? → Alert human
View case study
The AI role

Ranks riders and evaluates delivery offers against live availability and location.

The automation role

Moves the order through each stage, handles retries, and manages the fallback path without manual intervention.

Human role

Steps in only on full escalation, when no rider accepts.

Key idea

Automate the routine. Escalate the exception.

Outcome

A demonstration build showing reliable retry and fallback logic for dispatch; not yet deployed for a paying client.

Ecommerce · Automation · Revenue Recovery

Abandoned Checkout Recovery System

Problem

A Shopify store was losing potential revenue from unfinished checkouts because customers weren't being followed up automatically.

Solution

An automated recovery workflow built in n8n that detects unfinished checkouts, sends a reminder email, and logs the activity — no manual monitoring required.

Shopify → Unfinished checkout detected → n8n → Automated reminder email → Recovery logged → Google Sheets
Tools: Shopify · n8n · Email · Google Sheets
AI · Lead Generation · Outreach

AI Lead Generation System

Problem

Finding leads required hours of manually searching for businesses, checking websites, identifying gaps, and writing cold emails from scratch.

Solution

An AI-powered system that finds businesses, researches their sites, identifies gaps, and drafts personalized outreach — leaving the final decision to a human reviewer.

Find businesses → Research website → Identify gaps → Analyze opportunity → Generate outreach → Human review → Send
View case study
The AI role

Handles research and drafts personalized outreach based on identified business gaps.

Human role

Reviews every draft before it's sent — AI drafts, the human decides.

Key idea

Find the problem before writing the pitch.

Outcome

A repeatable research workflow replacing manual, one-off prospecting.
